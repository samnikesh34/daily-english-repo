Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Daily English Speaking ("we", "the app") is a small educational project that creates English-learning videos and posts them to our own accounts. This app is used only by its owner.

What we collect

When the owner connects the TikTok account, the app receives an access token that lets it upload our own videos as drafts. We do not collect, sell or share personal information about any other person. We do not collect data from viewers.

How we use it

The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private code repository and is used only to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account.

Sharing and retention

We do not share data with third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings, which makes the token unusable.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the Daily English Speaking channel page.
